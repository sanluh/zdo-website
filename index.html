From A to Z. Then zdo.

I tried all kinds of to-do and note-taking apps, from A to Z. None quite brought together the things I wanted. So I built zdo: z + do. Option Z.

A task often needs more than a one-liner. It needs the thinking behind it: a brief, a plan, research, or a few notes about what happens next. That’s why you can attach Markdown documents to your tasks and keep the context right beside the work.

A simple list is great for groceries. Real projects need more structure. Boards help you see how work is moving, and subtasks let you break it into pieces you can actually do. Zdo brings that structure together with your notes. And for the groceries? There are disposable Quick lists, too.

At home on Mac and iPhone

Zdo has a native macOS desktop app and a native iOS app. Plan projects and write on your Mac, then keep your tasks and notes close on your iPhone. Optional iCloud sync keeps your workspace together across your devices.

Your work stays yours

Zdo stores your workspace on your device. There is no Zdo account to create, and no advertising or third-party analytics in the app.

iCloud sync is optional. If you turn it on, your workspace and attachments are stored in your private iCloud database.
