How can we help?

Questions, feedback, or something that isn’t working? Get in touch.

zdo.support@icloud.com

When reporting a problem

Include your Zdo version, macOS version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Only send screenshots or files you are comfortable sharing; you do not need to send your full workspace.

Manage your subscription

Use Restore purchases in Zdo to restore access with the Apple Account used for purchase. You can review or cancel a subscription in Apple’s subscription settings.

Your data

You can export documents and create a workspace backup in the app. For details about local data, iCloud, and retained backups, read the Privacy policy.
